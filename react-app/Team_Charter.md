2. Existing App Summary

Find My Artisan is a web application that connects clients with skilled artisans who can provide services such as electrical work, plumbing, and other professions. The application is designed for clients looking for reliable artisans and for artisans who want to advertise their skills and previous work. Clients can search for artisans based on profession, location, and rating, view artisan profiles and availability, make and manage bookings, and review completed work. Artisans can manage their profiles, publish previous work as a portfolio, manage their availability, and update the status of jobs they complete. The application stores artisan, user, and job information to support the discovery, booking, and management of artisan services.

3. Part B Audit
Resources Stored by the Application
1. Artisan Information

The application stores:

Artisan name and personal information
Skills or abilities possessed by the artisan
Artisan location
Artisan availability
2. User Information

The application stores:

User personal and contact information
Type of job the user requires
Reviews of work completed by artisans
3. Job Information

The application stores:

Price incurred by the job
Success rate of the job
Type of job, such as electrical or plumbing
Photos and history of work completed
Bookings and their status:
Completed
Upcoming
Cancelled
User Actions

The application supports the following user actions:

Users can register, edit, and delete accounts for both artisans and clients.
Artisans can publish previous work as a portfolio.
Users can search for artisans using criteria such as profession, location, and rating.
Clients can view an artisan's availability and make bookings.
Clients can view the bookings they have made.
Artisans can mark completed work as completed.
Clients can rate an artisan's work.

Team Responsibility

The team will collaborate across frontend, backend, database, and coordination responsibilities to ensure that the Find My Artisan application correctly manages artisan information, user information, job information, bookings, portfolios, availability, and reviews while supporting the required user actions.
